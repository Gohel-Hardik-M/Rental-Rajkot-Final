Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rental Rajkot collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website, rental accommodation services, job listings, contact forms and related features.

Last updated: August 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Rental Rajkot is a Rajkot-focused online platform that helps users discover rental accommodation and job opportunities. We understand that information such as your name, email address, phone number and account information should be handled responsibly.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you use Rental Rajkot and explains how that information may be used to operate, maintain, improve and secure the platform.

Please note: Rental Rajkot does not ask users to submit passwords, payment-card information or other highly sensitive information through the public contact form. Please avoid submitting such information through forms or messages.

By using Rental Rajkot, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable laws and the actual features you use on the platform.

Information We Handle

Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with Rental Rajkot and which features you use.

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, account type and other information required for the account functionality.

Property Information

Property owners who submit listings may provide property, location, rental, facility and contact information needed to display and manage their listings.

Job Listing Information

Job-related information may include company names, job titles, descriptions, locations, experience requirements, skills, salary information where provided, application links and other information associated with job opportunities.

Contact Form Information

When you contact Rental Rajkot, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, subject and message so that we can review and respond to your enquiry.

Technical & Usage Information

When you visit a website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the website infrastructure, hosting provider, analytics services, advertising services, security systems or your browser.

Depending on the service involved, this information may include:

  • IP address or approximate network information.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Operating system information.
  • Pages visited and referring pages.
  • Approximate date and time of visits.
  • Basic website interaction and usage information.
  • Technical information used to detect errors, abuse or security issues.

This information is generally used to operate the website, understand website usage, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems and improve the user experience.

How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate purposes connected with operating and improving Rental Rajkot, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Displaying and managing rental property listings.
  • Displaying job opportunities and related application information.
  • Responding to contact enquiries and support requests.
  • Improving website functionality, content and search features.
  • Monitoring website performance and diagnosing technical problems.
  • Detecting suspicious, abusive or unauthorized activity.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory requirements where necessary.

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the platform without an appropriate basis for doing so.

Platform Information

Rental & Job Listing Information

Rental Listings

Rental listings may contain information provided by property owners or listing contributors, including property descriptions, locations, rent, facilities, availability information and contact details.

Rental Rajkot does not own or manage the properties displayed on the platform. Users should independently verify important property details, availability, rental terms and payment information directly with the relevant property owner.

Job Listings

Job listings may contain information obtained from employers, employer career pages or other sources used to publish employment opportunities.

Rental Rajkot does not itself become the employer merely because a job opportunity is displayed on the platform. Candidates should verify job descriptions, compensation, eligibility and application information directly with the relevant employer.

Information Submitted Through Contact Forms

If you submit a message through the Rental Rajkot contact form, the information you provide may be stored or processed so that we can review your enquiry and respond to you.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Subject of the enquiry
  • Message content

Important: Please do not submit passwords, banking credentials, payment-card numbers, authentication codes or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

Contact messages may be retained for a reasonable period necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain records, address disputes or improve customer support, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Rental Rajkot and certain third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies can be used for purposes such as:

  • Supporting website functionality.
  • Remembering certain preferences.
  • Understanding website usage.
  • Measuring website performance.
  • Supporting advertising functionality.
  • Security and fraud prevention.

You can usually manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Google Analytics

Rental Rajkot uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and to help us improve website performance, content and usability.

Google Analytics may process information about website visits, device information, approximate location, pages viewed, traffic sources and other usage information according to Google's applicable policies.

Analytics information is used primarily in an aggregated or analytical manner to understand website usage rather than to identify individual visitors for unrelated purposes.

Advertising & Google AdSense

Rental Rajkot may display advertisements through Google AdSense or other advertising services.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and improve advertisements. Depending on applicable settings and policies, advertising providers may use information associated with a browser or device to help determine which advertisements may be shown.

Rental Rajkot does not directly control the information collected or processed by third-party advertising providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Users can manage certain advertising preferences through Google's available advertising controls and their browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Rental Rajkot may rely on third-party services to operate certain parts of the website.

These may include services related to:

  • Website analytics.
  • Online advertising.
  • Website hosting and infrastructure.
  • Security and abuse prevention.
  • External employer career pages and application links.
  • Other technical services required to operate the platform.

When you follow a link to an external website, that website operates under its own privacy policy and terms. Rental Rajkot is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

When Information May Be Shared

Rental Rajkot does not intend to sell personal information simply because a user visits the website.

Information may nevertheless be accessible to or processed by appropriate parties when reasonably necessary to operate the platform, including:

  • Service providers supporting website infrastructure.
  • Analytics and advertising providers.
  • Authorized persons responsible for managing the platform.
  • Property owners when information is intentionally provided for a rental enquiry or listing-related interaction.
  • Employers or job listing providers where users choose to follow an application process or contact an employer.
  • Government authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Information may also be processed when necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents or violations of the platform's rules.

Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including operating user accounts, managing listings, responding to enquiries, maintaining security records, resolving disputes and complying with applicable legal obligations.

The actual retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is being retained.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or loss.

However, no website, database or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also protect their account credentials, avoid sharing passwords and use caution when communicating sensitive information online.

Your Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that information they submit to Rental Rajkot is accurate and appropriate for the feature being used.

  • Do not submit another person's information without appropriate permission or a lawful basis.
  • Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or authentication codes through public forms.
  • Do not use the platform to publish fraudulent or misleading information.
  • Verify rental information directly with property owners.
  • Verify employment information directly with the relevant employer.

Rental Rajkot may take reasonable action when information or activity appears to violate applicable law, platform rules or security requirements.

Children's Privacy

Rental Rajkot is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the services offered by the platform. If you believe that a child has submitted personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

External Websites & Links

Rental Rajkot may link to external websites, including employer career pages, social media platforms, application pages and other websites.

Once you leave Rental Rajkot and visit an external website, that website's own terms, privacy policy and data practices apply. We recommend reviewing the relevant website's privacy information before submitting personal information.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the information, you may have rights or choices concerning your personal information.

These may include requesting information about personal data associated with you, requesting correction of inaccurate information, or requesting deletion where applicable.

Requests can be sent to rentalrajkot21@gmail.com . We may need enough information to reasonably verify the request before taking action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Rental Rajkot may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal requirements or privacy practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page along with the updated date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
