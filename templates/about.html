Our Story

Built in Rajkot, for everyone looking for a place to call home here.

Rental Rajkot exists because finding a trustworthy hostel, PG or room in this city shouldn't mean scrolling through outdated ads or paying a broker just to see a photo.

How it started

A problem every Rajkot student knows

Every year, thousands of students arrive in Rajkot to study at RK University, Marwadi University, Atmiya University, Darshan University, VVP Engineering College, Government Engineering College and Saurashtra University. Almost all of them go through the same thing: a frantic search for a hostel or PG, usually starting with a WhatsApp forward or a faded notice board flyer near the college gate.

Listings were scattered across local Facebook groups, outdated directory websites, and word-of-mouth. Photos were old or missing entirely. Brokers charged a fee just to arrange a visit. And once you did find a room, there was no way to know if the place matched what was promised until you were already standing at the door with your bags.

Rental Rajkot was built to fix exactly that — one verified listing, one honest conversation with the owner, and one city at a time.
