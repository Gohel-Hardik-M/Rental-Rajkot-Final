About Rental Rajkot

A Rajkot-focused platform for finding a place to live and opportunities to grow.

Rental Rajkot helps people discover rental accommodation and job opportunities across Rajkot — from hostels, PGs and rooms to jobs for fresh graduates and experienced professionals.

Our Story

Built around everyday needs in Rajkot

Finding the right place to stay and finding the right opportunity to work are two important parts of starting or building a life in a city. Rental Rajkot was created to make both of these searches easier for people looking in Rajkot.

For students and working professionals, finding accommodation can involve searching through scattered listings, social media posts, local contacts and other sources. At the same time, fresh graduates and experienced professionals looking for employment opportunities often need to search across multiple company career pages and job sources.

Rental Rajkot brings these local discovery needs together in one Rajkot-focused platform. Users can explore available hostels, PGs and rooms for rent, while job seekers can discover employment opportunities from companies hiring in and around the city.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for people to find relevant local opportunities in Rajkot without making the search unnecessarily complicated.
